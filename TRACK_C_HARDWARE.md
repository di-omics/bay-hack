# Track C hardware and labware kit

## Bring if already available

- laptop and charger
- USB-C hub and common data cables
- phone charger or battery pack
- notebook and permanent marker
- digital calipers
- painter's tape or removable lab tape
- small ruler
- six to ten identical clean, empty screw-cap tubes
- matching rack for those tubes
- one spare cap and one intentionally damaged practice cap
- printed high-contrast fiducials on plain paper
- safety glasses if you already own a comfortable pair

Do not bring biological samples, chemicals, or mystery liquids. Use only clean,
empty labware unless the organizers explicitly approve a liquid and workspace.

## Best personal labware choice

Bring identical screw-cap cryovial-style or sample-storage tubes with ribbed
caps. Pick a size that can be held securely by a small parallel gripper. Bring a
rack that prevents tube rotation. The venue's own labware remains the first
choice if it is already modeled and compatible with the robot.

Avoid making the demo depend on one irreplaceable tube.

## Measure at the venue

| Measurement | Purpose |
|---|---|
| tube outer diameter | fixture bore |
| cap outer diameter | gripper width |
| exposed cap height | grasp pose |
| tube total height | approach and camera region |
| thread turns to release | wrist rotation |
| axial rise during release | coordinated lift |
| rack or deck mounting points | fixture mounting |
| camera distance and angle | cap-state calibration |

Record units in millimeters and degrees. Take each dimension twice.

## Fixture decision

Use this order:

1. Organizer-provided tube rack with adequate anti-rotation
2. Temporary clamp or taped mechanical stop approved by the host
3. Parametric printed fixture from `hardware/tube_nest.scad`

Do not spend the event waiting for a perfect print. A rigid, repeatable temporary
fixture is better than an elegant part that arrives after demo freeze.

## Optional liquid-handling beat

If the host supplies a compatible pipette or liquid handler, use an approved
colored water transfer after cap-off verification. Keep volumes large enough to
be visually obvious and within the supplied hardware's validated range. Use a
fresh tip. The core Track C claim does not require wet biology.
