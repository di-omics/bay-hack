# Event brief

Reviewed 2026-07-25 from the organizer email and official track pages.

## Track C: our primary track

Build a new robotic capability for the lab. The official directions include:

- open and close screw-cap tubes, cryovials, bottles, boxes, foil, and flexible
  packaging
- identify, reorient, sort, and transfer tubes
- build tools, fixtures, cap openers, suction tools, or end effectors
- operate lab equipment
- handle transparent, reflective, flexible, or deformable objects
- verify grasp, placement, cap removal, machine state, or liquid transfer
- retry, stop, or ask for help when verification fails
- recover from failed pickup, dropped items, rotated labware, and insertion
  failures
- build self-aligning or error-tolerant fixtures

TubeProof directly targets container handling, physical verification, failure
recovery, and self-aligning fixtures. It preserves the user's liquid-handling
strength by turning verified cap removal into a pipetting handoff.

## Announced Track C hardware

The official page lists YAM Arms, OpenArms, Piper Arms, UFactory xArm Lite 6,
Bambu H2D and X1C printers, cameras, labware, sensors, and Zeon's simulation and
control environment. Confirm the exact assigned arm, gripper, camera, and SDK at
the venue before integration.

## Track A fallback

Track A hunts for TEM-1 beta-lactamase inhibitors using cell-free expression,
nitrocefin kinetics, robotic liquid handling, and an adaptive round 2. The
complete Track A build remains in this repository. See
`OFFICIAL_TRACK_A_MATERIALS.md` and `TEM1_TRACK_A.md`.
